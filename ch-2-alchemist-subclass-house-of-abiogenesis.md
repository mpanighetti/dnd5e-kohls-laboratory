# Chapter 2: Alchemist Subclass: [House of Abiogenesis](https://github.com/mpanighetti/dnd5e-classes/blob/main/third-party/alchemist/house-of-abiogenesis.md)

_Create Life from Death_

On most worlds, the chemical and electrical interactions that resulted in the first spark of life is a secret lost to time, but practitioners of abiogenesis alchemy have learned how to reproduce this spark, creating new life from nonliving materials. Alchemists who have perfected this technique can augment their creations with powerful abilities using their potioncraft.

#### Abiogenesis Elixir

Your study of chemistry has granted you knowledge of the secret of abiogenesis, allowing you to grant life to nonliving substances. After you finish a Long Rest, you can touch a Small or Tiny corpse to transform it into a living creature known as an alchemist's monster. See the monster's full game statistics in the accompanying **[Alchemist's Monster](#alchemists-monster)** stat block.

If you spend 3 Alchemy Points while creating your monster, you can touch a Medium-sized object to create a Medium-sized alchemist's monster with Strength 17 (+3), and if you spend 5 Alchemy Points while creating your monster, you can touch a Large-sized object to create a Large-sized monster with Strength 19 (+4).

The monster is Friendly to you and your allies and obeys your commands. It reverts to the original intet object used in its creation if you die.

_**The Monster in Combat.**_ In combat, the monster acts during your turn. It can move and use its Reaction on its own, but the only action it takes is the Dodge action unless you take a Bonus Action to command it to take an action in its stat block or some other action. You can also sacrifice one of your attacks when you take the Attack action to command the monster to take the Attack action. If you have the Incapacitated condition, the monster acts on its own and isn't limited to the Dodge action.

_**Restoring or Replacing the Monster.**_ If your monster has died within the last hour, you can take a Magic action to touch it and expend a spell slot. The monster returns to life after 1 minute with all its Hit Points restored.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can create a new alchemist's monster by touching a Small or Tiny corpse. If you already have an alchemist's monster from this feature, the old one immediately reverts to the original inert object used in its creation. Anything the monster was wearing or carrying is left behind when it reverts to its inert form.

#### Level 7: Unnatural Strength

Your alchemical knowledge imparts increased potency to the creatures you create. An alchemist's monster you create has Advantage on Strength-based ability checks and Strength saving throws, counts as one size larger when determining its carrying capacity and the weight it can push, drag, or lift, and deals an additional 1d6 damage per size category above Small on any attack it makes.

#### Level 11: Monstrous Synergy

You have furthered your studies of creating life from nonlife, revealing methods of improving potioncraft imbibed by your creations. An alchemist's monster you create can now be under the non-instantaneous effects of two potions at the same time.

#### Level 15: Lifeblood Transfusion

The unnatural creature you have created can tap into its inner bodily reserves to convert itself into a potion as a self-sacrifice. As a Magic action, you can command your alchemist's monster to produce a potion from its body, without spending Alchemy Points and with the monster's body substituting for all ingredients. The potion can be any recipe that you are able to brew based on your Alchemist level. Additionally, when a creature drinks this potion, they regain a number of Hit Points equal to the monster's Hit Points when the new potion was created.

After your monster creates a potion in this manner, it is immediately reduced to 0 Hit Points, and the object used in its creation is destroyed.

Once you give this command, you regain the ability to do so after you finish a Long Rest.

### [Alchemist's Monster](https://github.com/mpanighetti/dnd5e-monsters/blob/main/special/alchemists-monster.md)

_Tiny, Small, Medium, or Large[^👹] Undead[^👹], Neutral_

**AC** 11 plus your Intelligence modifier

**HP** 4 plus five times your Alchemist level (the monster has a number of Hit Dice [d4s for Tiny, d6s for Small, d8s for Medium, or d10s for Large] equal to your Alchemist level)

**Speed** 40 ft.

**Skills** Athletics plus two skill proficiencies you possess of your choice

**Resistances** Necrotic

**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12

**Languages** one language of your choice that you speak

**CR** None (XP 0; PB equals your Proficiency Bonus)

#### Traits

_**Alchemical Nature.**_ The alchemist's monster is the same size and composition as the object used in its creation. A monster of size Medium or larger increases the reach of its Alchemical Strike by 5 ft. for each size category above Small. Additionally, the monster can only regain Hit Points by drinking a potion you brewed, by spending Hit Dice, or by finishing a Long Rest. When it drinks one of your potions, in addition to the potion's normal effects, it regains 1d6 Hit Points per Alchemy Point spent to brew the potion.

_**Covalent Bond.**_ Add your Proficiency Bonus to any ability check or saving throw the alchemist's monster makes.

_**Undead Fortitude.**_ If damage reduces the alchemist's monster to 0 Hit Points, it can make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is Radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the monster drops to 1 Hit Point instead.

#### Actions

_**Alchemical Strike.** Melee Attack Roll:_ Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, reach 5 ft.[^👹] _Hit:_ 3 (1d6) + 2 plus your Intelligence modifier Bludgeoning damage.

_**Lob Essence.**_ The alchemist's monster rips off a chunk of its own body and throws it at the target, dealing itself 3d6 Force damage. _Ranged Attack Roll:_ Bonus equals your spell attack modifier, range 20/60 ft. _Hit:_ The target takes Force damage equal to twice the amount the monster dealt to itself.

#### Reactions

_**Intercept (PB/Day).** Trigger:_ An ally that the alchemist's monster can see within its reach is hit by an attack. _Response:_ The monster swaps places with the ally (if willing) and becomes the new target of the attack. The original attack roll is compared to the monster's AC, potentially turning the hit into a miss.
